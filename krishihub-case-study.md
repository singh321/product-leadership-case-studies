# KrishiHub — AgriTech Supply Chain Platform

## Problem

Farmers face:
- demand uncertainty
- price volatility
- supply chain inefficiencies

---

## Goal

Build a demand-driven agricultural platform.

---

## Product Strategy

Use data + ML to:
- predict demand
- optimize pricing
- improve farmer outcomes

---

## Key Features

- demand prediction
- price intelligence
- crop advisory
- supply chain optimization

---

## Execution

- mobile-first platform
- strong on-ground adoption
- farmer onboarding at scale

---

## Impact

- significant revenue growth
- reduced wastage
- improved farmer income

---

## Key Learnings

- real-world ops matter as much as tech
- trust is critical in adoption
- data quality is foundational
