# DOCO — Retail Distribution Platform

## Problem

D2C brands struggle with offline distribution.

---

## Goal

Build a platform connecting brands with retailers.

---

## Product Strategy

- onboard retailers at scale
- enable repeat ordering
- optimize distribution network

---

## Key Features

- retailer onboarding
- ordering system
- supply chain coordination

---

## Challenges

- working capital intensity
- operational complexity
- logistics scaling

---

## Outcome

- strong revenue growth
- high repeat usage

---

## Learnings

- ops-heavy businesses are hard to scale
- unit economics matter deeply
