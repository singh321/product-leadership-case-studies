# Aasthy — Fractional Real Estate Platform

## Problem

Real estate investing is:
- capital intensive
- inaccessible to most users

---

## Goal

Enable small-ticket real estate investments.

---

## Product Strategy

- fractional ownership model
- digital-first experience
- trust-focused onboarding

---

## Key Features

- low investment entry
- digital documentation
- investor dashboard

---

## Key Decisions

- focus on trust (KYC, compliance)
- simplify investment experience
- design liquidity mechanisms

---

## Impact

- strong user adoption
- capital raised from retail investors

---

## Learnings

- trust > features in fintech
- compliance is product, not just backend
