# Product Decisions and Trade-offs

A collection of real product decisions and how I think about trade-offs.

---

## 1. Precision vs Recall (AI systems)

In many AI workflows:
- high recall increases noise
- high precision improves trust

My approach:
- optimize for precision in critical workflows
- increase recall gradually

---

## 2. Speed vs Platformization

- early stage → optimize for speed
- later stage → invest in platform

---

## 3. Automation vs Human Control

- full automation is risky early
- human-in-the-loop builds trust

---

## 4. Build vs Buy

- build for differentiation
- buy for speed and commodity capabilities

---

## 5. Product vs Ops complexity

Some businesses:
- look scalable
- but are ops-heavy underneath

Important to evaluate:
- unit economics
- operational burden

---

## Final Thought

Product leadership is not about perfect decisions.

It is about:
- making trade-offs consciously
- learning quickly
- adapting continuously
